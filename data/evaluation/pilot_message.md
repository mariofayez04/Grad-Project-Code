# Small pilot: what to send

Send this to 5 friends who did not help build the app. Ten minutes each is enough to give real numbers.

## Message (English)

Hi! I built a meal planning app for my graduation project and I need honest feedback. It takes about 10 minutes.

1. Open https://grad-project-code.vercel.app and choose New user. Please use a nickname, not your real name.
2. Fill in your details and tick your allergies. Write what you like or dislike in your own words.
3. Look at your plan. Tick the meals you would really eat, and press Swap a food or New meal on any you wouldn't.
4. On the Progress page, open "Rate this week" and answer honestly. Bad scores help me more than polite ones.
5. Fill in the attached sheet with 5 sentences, the way you'd tell an app what you can't eat, dislike or like. Please don't look at the app's examples first.

This is a student project, not medical advice. If you have a serious allergy or a health condition, please don't follow the plan. Just rate how it looks. You can delete your account afterwards from the Profile page.

Thank you!

## الرسالة (بالعربية)

أهلًا! عملت تطبيق لتخطيط الوجبات لمشروع التخرج، ومحتاج رأيك بصراحة. هياخد حوالي 10 دقايق.

1. افتح https://grad-project-code.vercel.app واختار New user. استخدم اسم مستعار، مش اسمك الحقيقي.
2. املأ بياناتك وعلّم على أي حساسية عندك. اكتب اللي بتحبه واللي مابتحبوش بأسلوبك.
3. شوف خطتك. علّم على الوجبات اللي هتاكلها فعلًا، ودوس Swap a food أو New meal على اللي مش هتاكلها.
4. من صفحة Progress افتح "Rate this week" وجاوب بصراحة. التقييم الوحش بيفيدني أكتر من المجاملة.
5. املأ الملف المرفق بخمس جمل، زي ما هتقول لتطبيق إيه اللي مش بتقدر تاكله أو مابتحبوش أو بتحبه. من فضلك ماتبصش على أمثلة التطبيق الأول.

ده مشروع طلابي ومش نصيحة طبية. لو عندك حساسية خطيرة أو حالة صحية، ماتمشيش على الخطة، قيّم شكلها بس. وتقدر تمسح حسابك بعدها من صفحة Profile.

شكرًا!

## Files to attach and what to do afterwards

- Attach `data/evaluation/heldout_sentences.xlsx`. Put the filled sheets back together in that same file.
- The ratings are saved on the hosted database, so tell your assistant when people have finished. The local evaluation reads `data/app.db` only, so the hosted ratings need to be exported first.
- Meal rating: send `data/evaluation/meal_rating_sheet.xlsx` to anyone qualified you know, such as a dietitian, nutrition student or doctor. Only report their job accurately.
